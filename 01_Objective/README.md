# 01 — Project Objective

## NYC Taxi Data Analysis & Insights

**Project Period:** 03/08/2025 — Present  
**Project Type:** Data Analytics Portfolio Project  
**Domain:** Transportation & Urban Mobility

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes New York City taxi trip data to uncover meaningful patterns and insights related to taxi demand, trip characteristics, fares, payment behavior, tipping, and geographic activity.

The objective is to transform raw transportation data into clear, data-driven insights that can support operational planning, service improvement, and better understanding of urban mobility patterns.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The primary objective is to develop a data-driven view of NYC taxi operations by analyzing trip-level data and identifying patterns that influence:

- Taxi demand
- Trip distance
- Trip duration
- Fare amounts
- Passenger behavior
- Payment methods
- Tipping behavior
- Pickup and drop-off activity
- Peak operating periods

The analysis will combine data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, visualization, SQL, and business intelligence techniques to produce a complete analytics workflow.

---

## 🔍 Key Analytical Objectives

### 01 — Demand & Timing

Identify the busiest periods by:

- Hour
- Day of week
- Month
- Peak vs. non-peak periods

### 02 — Trip Economics

Analyze the relationship between:

- Trip distance
- Trip duration
- Fare amount
- Total trip cost
- Additional charges

### 03 — Customer Behavior

Examine:

- Passenger counts
- Payment methods
- Average fares
- Tipping behavior
- Customer spending patterns

### 04 — Location Intelligence

Identify:

- High-demand pickup locations
- High-demand drop-off locations
- Popular travel routes
- Geographic patterns in taxi activity

### 05 — Data Quality

Assess and prepare the dataset by identifying:

- Missing values
- Duplicate records
- Invalid trips
- Extreme distances
- Unusual fares
- Potential outliers

### 06 — Business Insights

Translate analytical findings into practical insights that could support:

- Taxi fleet planning
- Driver allocation
- Demand forecasting
- Service optimization
- Urban transportation planning
